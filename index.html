How Amber Connect Works

1

Submit

Fill in the short form with your block, location, issue, email and phone number. Add a photo if helpful.

2

Get an ID

Your request receives a unique Complaint ID for future reference.

3

Track

An acknowledgement is emailed to you and the request is logged in real time.

4

Resolve

The manager takes action, marks it complete, and a closure update is emailed to you.

We're Test-Driving Amber Connect

We invite residents to use Amber Connect for their complaints and service requests. We're doing our best to make the system simple, responsive and effective. Your feedback will help us refine it and complete the circle.
